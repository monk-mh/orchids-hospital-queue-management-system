<user_request>Real-Time Queue Management in Hospitals
Context (India-specific):
In many Indian government and private hospitals, patients spend hours
waiting in long queues for registration, doctor consultation, or lab tests. With
no proper system to track their turn, patients face frustration, overcrowding,
and wasted time. Hospitals also struggle to manage multiple counters and
departments efficiently, leading to delays and patient dissatisfaction.
Hackathon Challenge:
Build a tech-driven queue management system (web/mobile app) that can:
Let patients view their token number and position in the queue remotely.
Send SMS/WhatsApp/app notifications when their turn is approaching
Allow hospitals to manage multiple counters, departments, and doctors
in real time.
Provide doctors and admins with a dashboard showing waiting patients,
completed consultations, and average waiting times.
Expected Outcome:
A prototype demonstrating real-time token/queue tracking.
A mobile/web app for patients to check their turn and receive alerts.
An admin/doctor dashboard to monitor and manage queues efficiently.
Optional features: patient feedback after consultation, analytics on peak
hours, or load balancing between counters</user_request>
